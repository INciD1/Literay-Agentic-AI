What does the indemnification clause in this contract actually cover?
Searching the document · search_document() •••
A
The indemnification clause (Section 9.3) covers two main scenarios: damage from misuse of the premises, and damage to a third party caused directly by the tenant. The landlord isn't liable for damage caused by force majeure.
Cited from Section 9.3
"The tenant agrees to indemnify all damages incurred by third parties arising from the tenant's actions...except in cases of force majeure."
